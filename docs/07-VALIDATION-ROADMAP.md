Working prototype with both instructor and student flows functional. Preparing for first real-world validation session with reference instructor (Steve) at the range.

## Validation Timeline

### Phase 1: Steve Validation (March-April 2026)
- Saturday session at the range: test mirror mode on iPad, test full instructor flow
- Iterate based on Steve's feedback (expect 2-3 weeks of intense iteration)
- Goal: Steve uses the app regularly with 3-5 real students for at least 2 weeks
- Key metric: Do students practice more/better between lessons?

### Phase 2: Second and Third Club (May-June 2026)
- Find 2-3 more clubs in Cataluna with active teaching programs
- Offer free 2-month pilot
- Channels: Steve's contacts (if any), direct outreach to clubs with golf schools
- Goal: Validate that the product works beyond Steve
- Key metric: Do other instructors adopt it? Do their students engage?

### Phase 3: First Paying Clubs (July-September 2026)
- Convert pilots to paying customers (EUR 300-400/month)
- Target: 3-5 clubs paying
- Document the case study with real metrics
- Key questions to answer: How do I acquire a new club (channel, cost, time)? What is 3-month retention? What is club LTV?

### Phase 4: Scale Preparation (October 2026+)
- With 5+ paying clubs and answers to the three questions above
- Decision point on Alessandro (or another commercial partner)
- Explore Madrid + Andalucia expansion
- Consider investment conversations with Speedinvest/NEA if metrics support it

## Saturday Session Plan (Steve)

### Block 1: Validate the Product
- Show mirror mode on iPad at 2-3 meters distance
- Ask: Is the semaphore visible and clear enough?
- Ask: Does the information shown match what you would correct in a beginner?
- Ask: What is missing?
- Show the full exercise creation flow
- Ask: Could you do this in under 2 minutes between students?
- Ask: Does the angle and evaluation configuration make sense to you?
- Record everything he says, especially criticism

### Block 2: Understand What Steve Wants
- Do NOT ask directly about partnership or money
- Ask: How do you imagine your students using this between lessons?
- Ask: Do you think other instructors would use something like this?
- Ask: How do you see the relationship between your physical machine and this app?
- Listen more than you talk
- Do NOT discuss pricing, do NOT make commercial promises, do NOT give timelines for "ready"
